## 2. Ms Li discovers that Bai used to have a job. A small tease keeps the conversation going.

- ID: bfb-work
- Purpose: Show surprise and ask how someone knows about you.
- Audio: [audio/shanghainese/work.mp3](audio/shanghainese/work.mp3) (original)

### Language cues

- **我还以为……** — I had assumed…; gently correct an earlier impression.
- **……你也晓得啊？** — You even know…? An incredulous follow-up; this cue follows the screen wording.

### Dialogue

**Ms Li**: 你老早上过班啊？

## 3. They talk about the direction her life has taken. She makes a self-deprecating joke; he offers a playful invitation.

- ID: bfb-downhill
- Purpose: Catch the double meaning, then respond without making the mood heavier.
- Audio: [audio/shanghainese/downhill.mp3](audio/shanghainese/downhill.mp3) (original)

### Language cues

- **下坡路** — Going downhill: both an easy descent and a life getting worse.
- **改天……好吧？** — Turn the image into a casual invitation for another day.

### Dialogue

**Ms Li**: 下坡路呀，能走得不顺吗？

**Bai**: 那改天我约你去爬山好吧。
Then let’s go hiking sometime, shall we?
